Chocolate Cake

A rich, soft, and delicious homemade chocolate cake.

Recipe Information

  • prep Time:20 minutes
  • cook Time:35 minutes
  • servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • butter

Instructions

  1. preheat the pan.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients.
  3. Add eggs and butter.
  4. pour into the baking pan.
  5. Bake for 35 minutes.
